What is TechBiter OS?

TechBiter OS is a modern Linux distribution designed to be fast, lightweight, and easy to customize. Whether you're a developer, a student, or just someone who loves tech, TechBiter OS gives you a clean and clutter-free environment to get things done. This comes with 50+ preinstalled dev tools.
